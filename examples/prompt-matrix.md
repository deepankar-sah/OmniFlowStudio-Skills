# OmniFlow Cinematic Prompting Formula

To achieve photorealistic, Hollywood-grade outputs in Veo 3.1 & Google Flow without distortion or plastic textures, structure your prompts using this 4-part syntax:

```text
[Camera Movement & Lens] + [Core Subject & Physical Action] + [Lighting & Atmosphere] + [Materiality & Color Grade]
```

---

## 1. Camera Direction Tags

Prefix your prompts with one of these camera movement anchors:
- `[Camera: drone-wide]`: Ultra-wide sweeping landscape or city scale.
- `[Camera: tracking shot]`: Lateral or backward walking follow shot with realistic parallax.
- `[Camera: slow push-in]`: Subtle emotional zoom into a character's face.
- `[Camera: low-angle tilt-up]`: Heroic framing looking up at a building, robot, or protagonist.
- `[Camera: handheld documentary]`: Natural organic micro-jitter with shallow depth of field.

---

## 2. Atmospheric & Lighting Modifiers

- **Golden Hour / Warm**: `warm sunset rim lighting, long directional shadows, golden atmospheric haze, dust motes dancing in sunbeams`.
- **Cyberpunk / Neon**: `drenched wet asphalt, neon pink and turquoise neon reflections, volumetric rain fog, specular puddle highlights`.
- **Cinematic Noir**: `high-contrast chiaroscuro, single key light cutting through cigarette smoke, deep crushed blacks, silver gelatin tone`.
- **Ethereal / Sci-Fi**: `bioluminescent cyan spores, soft diffused studio box light, sterile titanium and frosted glass reflections`.

---

## 3. High-Fidelity Materiality Tags

Replace generic adjectives like "high quality" with tactile physical materials:
- `micro-creases in weathered Italian leather`
- `brushed anodized aluminum with fine chamfered edges`
- `translucent subsurface scattering on skin`
- `35mm Panavision anamorphic lens flare, Kodak Vision3 500T color science`
